# Performance Inventory (pladdrr)

> **v4.9.19 note — read this before acting on the tables below.** This file is a
> *static* inventory (which files contain SIMD or threading), regenerated by
> `tools/perf_inventory.sh`. It says nothing about whether any of it pays off.
> For measured numbers see `dev/ASSESSMENT_2026-08-05.md`. Headlines:
>
> - **~94% of CPPS runtime is `SlopeSelector::getSlope_Siegel`** (repeated-median
>   trend fit). pocketfft is 0.3%; the parallelised cepstrogram smooth is 0.3%.
>   Optimising anything else in that pipeline is noise.
> - **The SIMD toggle moves whole-routine time by -1% to +6% on arm64.** SIMD pays
>   in the batch-statistics bridges (mean 4.1x, range 15.4x vs base R), not in
>   end-to-end analysis.
> - **Exception, worse than neutral: the SlopeSelector kernels.** Default-off since
>   v4.9.21 — they made CPPS 1.7x and AVQI 2.4x *slower* with bit-identical output,
>   because they vectorize the ~6% of `getSlope_Siegel` that is divides and leave
>   the ~94% that is `NUMquantile_e` untouched, while losing cross-TU inlining.
> - **Stock CRAN builds reach SSE2 on x86_64 and NEON on arm64 — never AVX2 or
>   AVX-512.** No `-march=` (CRAN policy) and no runtime dispatch.
> - Six `*_simd.cpp` files listed in earlier revisions of this inventory were
>   deleted in v4.9.19 for having no referenced symbols.
> - `std::nth_element` in `getSlope_Siegel` was measured and **rejected**: bit-exact
>   but 25% slower than Praat's median-of-ninthers. See `PRAAT_MODIFICATIONS.md`.
