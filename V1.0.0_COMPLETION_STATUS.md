### New Capabilities

**Cochleagram Object** - Basilar membrane auditory model
- `Sound$to_cochleagram()` - Standard filterbank method
- `Sound$to_cochleagram_edb()` - Ear-drum-brain model
- Query, transform, and export methods
- Bark frequency scale representation (0-25.6 Bark)

**Excitation Object** - Auditory nerve firing rate
- `Spectrum$to_excitation()` - From frequency spectrum
- `Cochleagram$to_excitation()` - From cochleagram
- Loudness modeling, perceptual distance, formant extraction
- ERB scale representation

### Applications Enabled

1. **Psychoacoustic Analysis** - Loudness and perception modeling
2. **Speech Perception Studies** - Auditory masking and processing
3. **Clinical Audiology** - Hearing loss simulation and testing

5. **Cochlear Implant Research** - Signal processing optimization
