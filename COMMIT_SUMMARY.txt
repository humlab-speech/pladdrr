# pladdrr v1.1.5 - Formant Extraction Fix Summary

## What We Fixed

**Problem**: `Sound$to_formant_burg()` segfaulted with "Polynomial_to_Roots not available"

**Solution**: Added missing dependencies + initialization code

**Result**: ✅ Formant extraction works perfectly (190 frames extracted from test.wav)

## Changes Made

## Known Limitations

Threading methods NOT supported:
- ❌ `to_formant_willems()` - crashes at 0x68 (threading issue)
- ❌ `to_formant_sl()` - crashes at 0x68 (threading issue)

Recommended methods:
- ✅ `to_formant_burg()` - DEFAULT, recommended by Praat
- ✅ `to_formant_keepall()` - Burg variant with all candidates
